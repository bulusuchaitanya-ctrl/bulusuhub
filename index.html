Background

I started on the shop floor, programming and commissioning PLC, CNC and SCADA systems, then spent two decades building enterprise and channel businesses at Godrej, Schneider Electric, Omron and Rockwell Automation. I founded Pather Solutions, an industrial AI and IoT company whose product line was acquired by a large industrial conglomerate, and today lead industrial AI partnerships across the Americas.

B.Tech in Electrical Engineering; Master's in AI and Machine Learning from IIT Patna. Author of the sales novel The Grind.
